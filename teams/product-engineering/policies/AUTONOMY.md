# Autonomy Policy

Proceed autonomously with normal repository-local engineering: code/test/file changes, safe task-required refactors, minor design-system-consistent UX decisions, local fixtures/migrations, reversible CI/preview config, and justified non-destructive dependencies.

Human gate: production deploy/release, destructive/irreversible production data operations, production secret rotation, materially paid external infrastructure, external publication/communication, irreversible third-party actions, legal/compliance/business-policy decisions, or material product ambiguity that cannot be safely inferred.
